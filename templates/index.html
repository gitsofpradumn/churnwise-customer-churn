Know About Customer Churn

Customer churn occurs when a customer stops using a company's service or ends their subscription.

Why customers leave

Pricing, poor service, unmet expectations, or better alternatives.

Why predict churn?

To identify potential risks early and prioritize customer support.

Business impact

High churn can reduce recurring revenue and increase acquisition costs.

A prediction is a decision-support signal. Businesses should validate it with customer feedback and real-world context.

About the Machine Learning Model

ChurnWise uses a Logistic Regression pipeline with preprocessing for numerical and categorical customer data. The reduced model uses six selected input features.

01 · Data Preparation

Handle missing values, scale numerical features, and encode categorical features within the pipeline.

02 · Train and Evaluate

Compare models using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC.

03 · Generate Prediction

Submit six customer features to the trained pipeline and return the predicted class and churn probability.

The six-feature model achieved 82.64% accuracy, 93.41% recall, and a 80.67% F1-score on the test comparison previously performed. Results may vary on new customer data.
